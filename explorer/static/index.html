WELCOME TO LLM EXPLORE

A small window into a model.

01

Choose your model.

Start in demo mode, load local Hugging Face weights, or connect a chat API. The capability badges show what each model supports.

02

Follow a neuron.

The atlas samples neurons across layers by their index. Select one, or use Go to neuron to open any index. Inspect signed activations on your text. The layout does not imply a circuit or semantic similarity.

03

Change one thing.

Compare an unchanged response with a response that scales one MLP neuron. 1× leaves it alone, 0× suppresses it, and 2× doubles it. Both runs use greedy decoding.

Demo labels, activity, and responses are synthetic. Live neuron labels are left unnamed until you investigate them. API connections provide chat only. Experiments are stored on this machine; API prompts go to your configured endpoint.
